How this forecast is calculated

Your opening balance is the starting point. Daily essentials and scheduled bills are deducted once per date. Expected income is added after that day’s expenses. This conservative order reveals shortfalls that an end-of-day balance can hide.

Both the start and end date are included. A forecast can cover up to 366 days. Imported transactions describe past spending; they do not change your balance or create scheduled bills. Do not list a cost twice. Future income may arrive late, so test a later date in your plan.

All balances are calculated by application code. AI interpretations, when available, are shown separately with the figures they reference.

Supported message format

One transaction per line, with an explicit date. Supports the simplified sample and dated alerts with “INR/Rs amount debited/credited” or “debited by/credited with INR/Rs amount”, followed by an “on” date and a “to/from” counterparty.

Examples:

2026-10-29 INR 180.00 debited to Campus Canteen ref DEMO001Rs. 1,250.50 debited from A/c XX1234 on 03-11-2026 to VPA cafe@upi UPI Ref 123456789012

Alert dates may use YYYY-MM-DD, DD-MM-YYYY or DD/MM/YYYY. These are documented pattern examples, not a claim of support for every bank. Alerts with missing dates, failed/pending payments or collect requests are rejected. Mark your own-account transfers during review. The simplified format also supports “credited from” and “transferred to”. An optional reference follows “ref”. This edition does not read PDFs or every bank’s SMS format.
