How this forecast is calculated

Your opening balance is the starting point. Daily essentials and scheduled bills are deducted once per date. Expected income is added after that day’s expenses. This conservative order reveals shortfalls that an end-of-day balance can hide.

Both the start and end date are included. A forecast can cover up to 366 days. Existing transactions are already reflected in your starting balance. Do not list a cost twice. Future income may arrive late, so test a later date in your plan.

These are deterministic estimates from your inputs. AI explanations and transaction import are planned for later phases.
